02 — What they share共同的原则

Different jobs.
The same three habits.不同的工作，
同样的三个习惯。

01

Ask before guessing先问，再动手

An ambiguous request gets a question, not a confident wrong answer. What they could not find, they say they could not find.含糊的请求换来一个问题，而不是自信的错答案。查不到的，就说查不到。

02

A person signs off关键的事由人签字

Spending, filing, prescribing, exporting, opening a pull request — anything that costs or can’t be undone waits for a named person to say yes.花钱、提交、开处方、导出、开合并请求——凡是要花钱或无法撤回的，都等一个具体的人点头。

03

Keep the record留下记录

Who asked, which tool ran, what changed, whether it was checked. Close the laptop mid-task and they pick up where the work actually got to.谁提的、哪个工具跑的、改了什么、有没有核对。任务做到一半合上电脑，回来从真正做到的地方继续。
