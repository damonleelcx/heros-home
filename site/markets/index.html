How to read a call如何看懂涨跌判断
▲Dow道指54% chance up涨的概率 54%

The desk thinks there is a 54% chance the Dow closes higher that day than the day before — and a 46% chance it closes lower. It is a probability, not the size of the move.市场观察认为道指当天收盘高于前一天收盘的概率为 54%，收低的概率为 46%。这是概率，不是涨跌幅。

  • 50% is a coin flip. The further from 50%, the stronger the call. Before a release most calls sit between 50% and 60%.50% 相当于抛硬币，离 50% 越远判断越强。数据公布前，多数判断在 50%–60% 之间。
  • ▲ up / ▼ down is the more likely direction for that index. – No clear lean means 48–52%: no real edge either way.▲ 涨 / ▼ 跌是该指数更可能的方向。– 无明显倾向表示 48%–52%，两边都没有真正的优势。
  • Before the open (about 9:20 AM ET) the call for that day is re-read from index futures — the strongest guide there is to the day’s close, tested in the calendar view — and then locked at 9:30. Days further ahead have no such signal, so most of their calls sit near 50%.开盘前（美东约上午 9:20）会根据股指期货重新判断当天走势——这是收盘方向最有力的指引，回测见日历视图——然后在 9:30 锁定。更远的日期没有这类信号，所以大多接近 50%。
  • Low / medium / high impact is how much this kind of event usually moves that index.低 / 中 / 高影响表示这类事件通常对该指数影响多大。
  • Call for the day combines all of that day’s events into one call per index. A report after the 4 PM ET close counts toward the next trading day, when the market first trades on it.当日判断把当天所有事件合并成每个指数一个判断。美东下午 4 点收盘后发布的财报计入下一个交易日，因为市场在那时才首次反映。
  • Every day’s call is locked before the open and checked against the close: see the calendar’s ✓ / ✗ and the track record.每天的判断在开盘前锁定、收盘后核对：见日历中的 ✓ / ✗ 与历史命中。
